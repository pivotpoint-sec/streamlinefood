From the farm to your warehouse

Sourcing, packaging and shipping

We handle every step between Nigerian growers and your shelves, so you deal with one team instead of farmers, packers and freight agents.

Sourcing you can rely on

We work with farmers, processors and market traders across Nigeria. Every batch is inspected before we accept it: palm oil for colour and clarity, garri and flours for dryness and fineness, beans for sorting, and dried seafood for cleanliness and proper drying.

Produce that doesn't meet the grade is rejected, so what reaches you is consistent from one order to the next.

What each service covers

Sourcing

  • Buying direct from growers and processors
  • Batch inspection and grading
  • Seasonal supply planning for regular buyers

Packaging

  • Cleaning, sorting and sealing
  • Bottles, pouches, sacks, jerrycans and drums
  • Streamline or private-label branding

Shipping

  • Delivery across Nigeria
  • Export freight by air or sea
  • Export documentation and customs paperwork

How ordering works

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send the products, quantities and destination through the quote form or WhatsApp.

  2. Get your quote

    We confirm price, pack sizes and delivery time before you pay anything.

  3. We source and pack

    Your order is bought, quality-checked, packed and labelled in Abuja.

  4. We ship it to you

    You get updates and shipping documents until the goods arrive.
