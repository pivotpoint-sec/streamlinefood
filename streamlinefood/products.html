Questions buyers ask

Is there a minimum order?

Retail customers can order single packs. For bulk and export orders, send your list and we will tell you the most cost-effective quantity for shipping.

Can you pack under my own brand?

Yes. We offer private-label packing with your logo and product information on bottles, pouches, bags or cartons.

Do you ship outside Nigeria?

Yes. We arrange export freight by air or sea and prepare the documents your shipment needs. Tell us your destination when you request a quote.

How do I pay?

Once you accept the quote, we send payment details. Payment terms for repeat and bulk buyers can be agreed on request.
