From the farm to your warehouse

One partner for the whole journey, so you deal with a single team instead of farmers, packers and freight agents.

Sourcing

We buy from farmers, processors and markets across Nigeria and check every batch for cleanliness, dryness and grade before we accept it.

Packaging

Produce is cleaned, sorted and sealed in food-safe packs, bottles, sacks or drums, labelled under the Streamline brand or your own.

Shipping

We arrange delivery within Nigeria and export freight abroad, including the paperwork your consignment needs to clear customs.

How ordering works

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send the products, quantities and destination through the quote form or WhatsApp.

  2. Get your quote

    We confirm price, pack sizes and delivery time before you pay anything.

  3. We source and pack

    Your order is bought, quality-checked, packed and labelled in Abuja.

  4. We ship it to you

    You get updates and shipping documents until the goods arrive.
