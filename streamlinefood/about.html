About Streamline

Good food, great drinks and a healthier tomorrow.

Who we are

Streamline Food & Beverages Ltd. is a Nigerian company based at Kubwa Market in Abuja. We source everyday staples from Nigerian farms and markets, package them to a consistent standard, and deliver them to households, shops, restaurants and importers.

Our aim is simple: make it easy to buy genuine, good-quality Nigerian food, whether you are in Abuja or abroad and missing the taste of home.

Quality first
